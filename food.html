Flavor of the Himalayas

Nepalese Cuisine

Dal Bhat

The iconic staple meal of Nepal, served with rice, lentil soup, vegetables, and pickles.

Momo

Steamed dumplings filled with meat or vegetables, often served with a spicy dipping sauce.

Thukpa

A warming noodle soup flavored with herbs, vegetables, and sometimes chicken or beef.

Taste the tradition

Simple food, rich flavor

Nepalese food is as varied as its landscapes, combining fresh ingredients, local herbs, and comforting spices. Meals often reflect the seasons and the region, with a balance of warmth, nutrition, and flavor.
