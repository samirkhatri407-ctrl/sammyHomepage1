About Nepal

Land of diversity

Where the Himalayas meet heritage and hope

Nepal is a country of dramatic beauty and remarkable diversity. From the towering Himalayan peaks to the warm valleys, each region carries its own language, tradition, and story. It is a place where ancient customs remain alive, and where nature and spirituality shape daily life.

The country is home to people of many ethnic groups, religions, and languages, yet they share a common spirit of hospitality, resilience, and harmony. Nepal's history is shaped by mountains, pilgrimage routes, and centuries of cultural exchange.
