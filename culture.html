Culture of Nepal

🎊

Festivals

Dashain, Tihar, and Holi bring communities together in music, color, prayer, and joyful celebration.

🪷

Religion

Hinduism and Buddhism shape the spiritual heart of Nepal and influence its daily rituals and architecture.

💃

Arts

Traditional dance, folk songs, and beautiful handicrafts reflect the creativity of Nepal’s communities.

Sacred living

Temples, monasteries, and everyday rituals

Nepal’s heritage is visible in every corner of its towns and villages. Sacred courtyards, prayer flags, temples, and monasteries enrich the landscape with both spiritual depth and artistic beauty.

Festivals are not simply events here—they are shared experiences of gratitude, tradition, and togetherness. Music, ritual, traditional attire, and warm hospitality are all powerful expressions of Nepali identity.
